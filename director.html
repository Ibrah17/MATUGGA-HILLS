Director's Message

Welcome to Matugga Hills Secondary School - a community committed to excellence, character, and opportunity.

From the Desk of the Managing Director

Mr. Arinaitwe Kenneth - Managing Director

It is my pleasure to welcome you to Matugga Hills Secondary School. Since our beginning, we have worked to build a school where learners receive quality education in an environment that values discipline, faith, responsibility, and respect.

Our vision is to develop confident and capable young people who are ready to serve their families, communities, and country. We believe that every learner has potential, and that consistent guidance, committed teachers, and supportive families help that potential grow.

"Every learner deserves the opportunity, guidance, and encouragement to become the best version of themselves."

As a school, we continue to strengthen academic learning while giving students opportunities in sports, music, dance, drama, leadership, and service. These experiences help learners discover their gifts and develop the character needed for lasting success.

Thank you for trusting Matugga Hills Secondary School. We welcome you to join our school community as we continue building excellence, one little thing at a time.

Yours sincerely,
Mr. Arinaitwe Kenneth
Managing Director
