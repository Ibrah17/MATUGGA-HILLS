Headteacher's Message

Welcome to Matugga Hills Secondary School — a place of learning, character, and community.

From the Desk of the Headteacher

Mr. Kato Edward — Head Teacher

To all our visitors, subscribers, and viewers: I greet you in the mighty name of God. I am honoured to serve you at Matugga Hills Secondary School, which began more than ten years ago with a vision to improve learners' livelihoods, nurture their spirituality, and restore their self-confidence.

Our commitment to academic excellence has been steady and measurable; each year our students achieve stronger results because of focused teaching, engaged learners, and supportive families. However, we believe that education is more than grades. We emphasise discipline, moral character, and community service, because true success is demonstrated by how graduates contribute to society.

"Discipline and faith create the foundation upon which knowledge builds lasting success."

We support daily prayer across denominations and provide spaces for Christians and Muslims to worship respectfully. Beyond the classroom, Matugga Hills trains the whole child — sports, music, dance, drama, and a variety of clubs help students discover talents and build confidence.

When you choose Matugga Hills Secondary School, you choose a community that values academic rigour, spiritual growth, discipline, and commitment. We welcome you to join us on this journey of transformation.

Yours sincerely,
Mr. Kato Edward
Head Teacher
