School Motto

“It's a little thing that matters”

We believe that great success is built on small, consistent actions — integrity, kindness, and diligence in every detail.

Our Vision

To be a school of excellence in developing all students academically, socially, emotionally, and spiritually to compete and contribute in a global society.

Core Values

  • Integrity – We all live as proof of moral principles
  • Respect – We treat everyone with courtesy and honour
  • Commitment – We show commitment to our students
  • Responsibility – We are trustworthy, reliable and accountable
  • High Expectation – We all set high goals, inspire personal best and continuous improvement of all staff and students

WHY JOIN US

Your Future Starts Here

At Matugga Hills Secondary School, we are committed to providing a holistic environment where every student can discover their purpose and achieve academic greatness.

  • Academic Excellence

    We provide a robust curriculum designed to challenge students and foster critical thinking skills.

  • Strong Community

    Our school is a family where every student is supported, valued, and encouraged to thrive together.

  • Spiritual Values

    We nurture character and integrity, grounding our students in values that guide them for a lifetime
